*GradeUp – Tertiary Study Assistant Platform*

*Project Overview*

GradeUp is a full-stack learning platform designed to provide tertiary students with a simple and smart way to understand and pass their courses.

*Project Goal*

The goal of GradeUp is to help university students who struggle with voluminous and complex course handouts. The platform allows students to upload their course handouts as PDFs, automatically summarizes them into simple key points, and generates practice questions from different angles to improve understanding and exam performance.

*Core Features*

- User Login / Signup
- Upload PDF Handouts
- Summarize to Key Points
- Generate Practice Questions
- Practice / Quiz Mode
- My Courses Library
